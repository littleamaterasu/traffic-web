Else `const reader = response.body.getReader()`, loop `reader.read()`, accumulate `Uint8Array` chunks and a running byte total; the moment total > MAX_RESPONSE_BYTES call `await reader.cancel().catch(() => undefined)` and throw response_too_large; after done, concatenate chunks and decode with `new TextDecoder("utf-8")` (streaming concat into one Uint8Array, then decode once). Failures (including scraper_busy) stay uncached because the store never caches rejections - assert this in tests.
